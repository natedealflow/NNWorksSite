How a deal works

Plain steps, no surprises.

  1. Intro call. 30 minutes. No documents needed.
  2. Mutual NDA. Signed before any numbers change hands.
  3. Light data request. Three years of financials, your contract list and key staff.
  4. Indication of value. As fast as you want; I aim for two weeks.
  5. Letter of intent. Price, structure and timing in writing.
  6. Diligence and financing. Financial review, contract and certification review, lender approval.
  7. Closing and transition. You stay on as a consultant for an agreed period to hand off relationships.

Common questions

What owners usually ask first.

Will my contracts survive a sale?

It depends on the contract and the deal structure. Some sales need a government novation, and size and SDVOSB status must be recertified after certain transactions. I plan this with you and your counsel before anything is signed.

Do you keep my team?

That is the plan. The business depends on your people, and I do not buy companies to cut them.

How do you pay?

With my own money alongside bank financing. I am pre-approved with several banks and lenders, and the mix depends on deal size. A seller note can be part of it when it helps both sides.

Will you buy the real estate?

Often, yes. If you own the property, I can buy it with the company or lease it from you, whichever works better for both sides.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. I sign an NDA with you before you share financials, and I never contact your customers or employees without your permission.

I'm a broker or advisor. Do you honor fee agreements?

Yes, in writing, before I see the deal.

Are you a private equity firm?

No. I am a veteran owner-operator buying a company to run and grow.
